A procedural newspaper front page summing up 19 November 2026 in a single 1024x1024 image, drawn entirely by code — no photographs, no downloads, no randomness. The lead story is the first-ever trilateral meeting of Trump, Xi and Putin in Shenzhen at the APEC summit, followed by the Crimea bridge being hit again, the U.S. and Iran trading fire across the Gulf, Nvidia halting China chip shipments, a Van Gogh selling for $190.4M, Anthropic eyeing a $1.4 trillion debut, the Myanmar genocide verdict, tanks crossing the ceasefire line in Gaza, and the EU's €45BN Ukraine loan, with a ticker carrying GTA VI's worldwide rollout, Cyclone Akvan nearing India, Fed minutes pointing to more hikes, Prague marching for free press, Voyager 1 passing a light-day, the $2BN Philippines loan, and Stacy Keach dying at 85. Every headline, pictogram, flag, and rule line is produced by a hand-rolled raster canvas, a small 5x7 bitmap font, and a hand-written PNG encoder in a single TypeScript file.

Run it with:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
